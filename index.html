I’m a business data analyst in Chicago, with more than ten years in data. I sit between the business and its data: I find out what people need to know, then find it, build it and make sure it can be trusted.

What I Do

Ten years is long enough to have worked on every part of the journey data takes. Here is what that has looked like.

It Starts with the Business

Before any data work, I sit down with the people who need it. Requirements, workshops and conversations with executives are where I begin, whether I’m onsite with a client or leading an offshore team of up to 15.

ETL

Getting data from where it lives to where it’s needed. At Central Garden & Pet I delivered a Salsify-to-Insite data load in record time at half the cost, and at Nedbank I designed how data flows from the bank’s data reservoir into its data marts.

Data Warehousing

Designing the models that reports sit on. I modeled Nedbank’s Enterprise Data Mart and CIB Mart to the IFW framework, and built the canonical data model behind Central Garden & Pet’s cost and profitability reporting.

Data Engineering

When the analysis needs a tool that doesn’t exist, I build it: a versioned catalog warehouse, a search layer over 150+ code repositories, and a converter for legacy SQL.

Data Governance

Lineage, controls, data quality and privacy that hold up in an audit. I’ve done this for Royal Bank of Canada’s regulatory reports, for protected health data at Blue Cross Blue Shield Association, and for Nedbank’s Basel III reporting.

AI

These days I lead how AI fits into all of the above. I build AI agents that trace lineage from source code, assign business terms and write data quality rules, with every answer traceable to its evidence.

A Few Recent Projects

These are three of the AI projects I’ve led. Each has a full write-up.

  • Automated Data Lineage from Source Code

    Regulators and data owners needed to know where critical data comes from, but the documentation was manual and out of date. I automated the answer by reading the code itself.

    94% recall at 97% precision

  • Business-Glossary Term Assignment

    Tens of thousands of fields had no business terms, and manual tagging was inconsistent. I built an explainable way to assign them, with data stewards kept in the loop.

    Months of tagging, done in hours

  • Data Quality Rules Written by AI

    Data quality rules were written by hand and often pointed at the wrong tables. I led an AI approach that writes them from plain-English business rules and recorded lineage.

    SQL accuracy up from 82% to 91%
