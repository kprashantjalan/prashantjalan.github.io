A Bit About Me

I’m a business data analyst with more than ten years across ETL, data warehousing, data engineering, data governance and AI.

My job has always been to understand what the business needs to know, find the data that answers it, and make sure the answer can be trusted. I’ve gathered requirements with business teams, designed data models and data marts, built Power BI and Tableau dashboards, traced regulatory reports back to their sources, and presented the results to executives up to the C-level.

I spent eight years as a consultant, which taught me to get up to speed quickly in a new client, a new industry and a new team. I’ve worked onsite in South Africa, led offshore teams of up to 15, and delivered in both Agile and Waterfall settings as a Certified Scrum Product Owner.

More recently I’ve led how AI fits into this work, building AI agents and tools that make analysis faster while keeping every answer traceable to its evidence.
